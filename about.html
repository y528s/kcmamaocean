Water moves. Water softens. Water holds.

Mama of three who somehow found herself far from the California ocean that raised her — but I've carried that water with me.

My path has moved through education, motherhood, childbirth and breastfeeding education, doula and postpartum work, integrative health coaching, yoga, meditation, women's circles, and body-centered practices.

Different chapters. Same calling: helping people feel more at home in themselves.

My roots

The medicine of holding space.

At the heart of my roots is the profound bond I share with my grandmothers. They helped shape the way I understand love, care, nourishment, and what it means to truly hold another person.

They taught me the medicine of gathering around a table, offering nourishing food, listening, and creating a home where someone feels safe enough to exhale.

That wisdom lives in the way I mother, nurture, and show up for others. Different generations. Different chapters. The same medicine flowing through.
