Welcome

Soften. Release. Reconnect. Flow.

I'm Daniella — a California-born, double-water-sign mama, educator, somatic and trauma-informed integrative health coach and bodyworker.

Water has always felt like home to me. I believe our bodies are a lot like water: we are meant to move, feel, soften, release, and flow.

Together, we create space to slow down, reconnect with your body, and listen to the wisdom it has been carrying all along.
